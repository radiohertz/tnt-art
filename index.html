F. F. Bruce

Frederick Fyvie Bruce (1910–1990) was a Biblical scholar and one of the founders of the modern evangelical understanding of the Bible. His work The New Testament Documents: Are They Reliable? is considered a classic in the discipline of Christian apologetics.
